# Future game ideas

Recorded September 14, 2026. Planning backlog; these games are not implemented.

## Direction

Add simple, familiar games to the future home screen for quick boredom breaks. Aim for six lightweight games with easy-to-understand rules and quick replays.

## Simon Says — requested idea

Turn the existing demo colors into an interactive memory game on the future home screen. Flash a sequence of colors, then have the player tap the colors in the same order. Add one step after each successful round; a mistake ends the run and offers a quick retry. Show the longest pattern completed as the score.

Preserve the playful novelty of making the demo colors something people can actually play with.

## Flappy Bird style game — requested idea

Tap to keep a bird in the air and guide it through gaps between pipes. Score each gap passed, end the run on a collision, and offer a quick retry. Add this alongside Simon Says and the four suggested classics.

## Four additional classic games — suggestions

The request is for four more similarly simple classics; the selections below are proposals for future consideration.

1. **Memory Match:** Flip cards and find matching pairs. Use a small board for short sessions and count moves.
2. **Whack-a-Mole:** Tap targets as they appear during a short timed round. Score successful hits.
3. **Snake:** Steer a snake toward food, grow with each bite, and avoid the walls and your own tail.
4. **Sliding Puzzle:** Slide numbered tiles into order on a small board. Track moves and allow an easy restart.

Keep entry and replay fast, instructions brief, and touch controls simple. These are future ideas, not a request to change the current home screen or demo behavior now.
