## October 11 saved-recipient reuse and targeted recovery

Workflow `2026-10-11.6` supersedes the `.5` mandatory four-surface inventory. This is a Pulse operational rule; no live Remitly screen was inspected here. On the correct signed-in Business account with a fresh owned browser lease and current withdrawal attempt/version, use this decision:

1. Normal first-time preparation: use Pulse's first-time label and the requested recipient. Find a matching corrected saved contact in Settings → Contacts and reuse it; if none exists, create the recipient only after the Pulse preparation claim. A contact is neither prior payment nor a saved draft. In the existing contact's **Send money** flow, an empty amount form is where preparation begins, not an old draft or a blocker.
2. After an interruption/error: inspect the known draft review URL if one exists and refreshed Home resume/draft/Ready to review cards. Inspect Scheduled transfers only if a schedule was attempted or a known schedule reference exists. A verified saved `first_time_draft` is reopened and completed on the **same attempt** through guarded `record_preparation.recovery`; never recreate it.
3. If no draft is found, inspect the saved contact. For a matching corrected contact, call the existing guarded `pulse_payout_retry_unsaved_preparation` with `noDraftSaved=true`, `noRecipientSaved=false`, `reuseSavedRecipient=true`, `recipientMatches=true` and other factually supported no-effect flags. If no contact exists, use `noRecipientSaved=true` and create it after the fresh claim. A mismatch gets its actual reason; a matching contact is routine reuse, not a blanket blocker. `saved_recipient_reuse_required` means the matching proof is missing. No stable provider contact ID or hypothetical hidden draft screen is required.
4. Inspect complete relevant Activity/transfer history if sending, funding or link issuance was crossed **or its boundary is uncertain**. A sent/pending/uncertain transfer, debit, issued link or human-release marker goes to existing outcome investigation. For a known pre-send boundary with no contradictory evidence, use the attempt/audit action log and relevant draft view for truthful no-effect evidence; do not invent a bank-portal or global-history requirement. If a required target view fails, retry its read-only load once when renewable, then report the exact view/failure/next action.

Keep `attemptId`/`expectedVersion`, exact reservation/snapshot, owned lease, fresh `observationId`/`sourceUrl`/`observedAt`, protected evidence/historyCoverage and all backend guards. `recipientRecordInspected=true` follows the actual contact check even when a contact is found. `previousDraftClosed=true` means no actual saved draft exists and the old open form is inactive/left, never that a provider record was canceled. The guarded retry cancels only the old failed INTERNAL attempt, retains the same withdrawal and coins, returns `awaiting_quote`; Maker immediately calls `prepare` without `quoteHash`. When the response says `recipientHandling=reuse_existing`, use **that contact**, enter the amount, obtain the quote, verify the actual autosaved/reopened first-time draft, report final quote plus draft, and read back ready for independent review. Do not create/edit another contact, fabricate an ID, issue a link, confirm funding or send. The human alone makes the final send decision. The older `.5` global-inventory rule is superseded.
